Artificial intelligence has become vital to nations, governments, and large corporations. Many of these large organizations are implementing Sovereign AI systems, which will provide them with artificial intelligence services, including infrastructure, data, models, and a trained workforce combined with the security and data privacy features that will enable them to achieve their agenda. A sovereign AI solution is one that is fully contained within an entity, such as a company or country, and enables the organization to meet its objectives while maintaining security and privacy.
